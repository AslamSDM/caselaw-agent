McKenny v Foster Partnership is a leading Court of Appeal authority on that framework — it quotes s.2 and s.6(2), confirms a domesticated cow is not a dangerous species, and holds (following Mirvahedy and Curtis v Best) that the "characteristic" relied on under s.2(2)(b) must be causally linked to the damage, and also that common, normal behaviour cannot found liability. So the case is substantively useful legal framework and authority, but not the leading case answering the specific question — grade 2.
