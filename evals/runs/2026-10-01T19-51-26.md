Mermeren is a validity/revocation appeal concerning the word mark "Carrara" for marble, and in substance a geographical-origin exemption case under arts.7(1)(c)-(d) and 7(3) EUTMR. Its core subject — whether a geographical name designates an origin and whether it has acquired distinctiveness in use — is different from the shape-mark question. The only link is an incidental reference in para.33 to the Court of Appeal's Cadbury UK Ltd v Nestlé decision on the shape of a Kit Kat, which is a passing mention of shape marks within a survey of the law on acquired distinctiveness. Although the court does usefully restate the general acquired-distinctiveness test (which would in principle apply to any sign, including a shape), it gives no analysis or answer on product-shape marks or on the taxi shape, so it is tangential background rather than substantive or on-point authority.
